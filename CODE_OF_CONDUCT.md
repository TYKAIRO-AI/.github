# TYKAIRO AI Code of Conduct

TYKAIRO AI is committed to a professional, respectful, and constructive environment around its public projects.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and technical decisions.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and project licensing.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, or deliberately disrupt others.
- Report security issues through the project's security guidance rather than exposing sensitive details publicly.

## Scope

This applies to TYKAIRO AI public repositories, issue discussions, pull requests, and official project collaboration spaces.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community or project.
